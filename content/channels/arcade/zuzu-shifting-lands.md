    Play the five-land illustrated expedition. Draw or choose a reachable road, pick how you answer each encounter, face each land's major trial, use earned supplies, and read the journal of changing places. Each choice's dice chip reads like "Risky 7": how hard the check is, then what your two dice must show once your skill is added. Tap the chip for the sum. Skills on the character sheet reflect your earned history; saved dice keep the rules of the expedition they were rolled in. Use Homestead workshop to visit the original separate prototype. Each premade traveler has their own cards, choices and three endings to find for the scrapbook.

Choose one of thirteen travelers, from Zuzu the ronin to a Dustwater barkeep, a runaway novice and a Storm Crow witch, or make your own, and cross five shifting lands on an illustrated board: 120 painted encounter cards, each traveler's own cards and endings, five major trials and a saved journey you can resume. The game is open for playtesting and feedback.
