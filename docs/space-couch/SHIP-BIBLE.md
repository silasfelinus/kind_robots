# Space Couch: Vessel and visual continuity bible

**Display title:** Space Couch. **Stable IDs:** `game-of-captains`, route `/play/game-of-captains`, save key `game-of-captains-voyage-v1`. Former display title `This Means Warp` survives only as historical context.

## Non-negotiable ship silhouette

The spaceship IS one exceptionally long **red upholstered couch**. It is not a space cruiser with a sofa inside. Travelers ride on the cushions in open space. An almost invisible **force shield** encircles the couch and maintains breathable air, pressure, warmth, and safety in the vacuum. No traditional hull, cockpit, command bridge, ship deck or shuttle should appear where the couch belongs.

**Miniaturization tech:** One temporary room can unfold **behind** the backrest, using pocket-space engineering. The couch stays visible from the front. Examples: holographic rehearsal holodeck, diplomatic war room, medical infirmary, emergency bunk, zero-gravity laboratory. Rooms are impossibly larger inside than their folded volumes. They do not replace the couch or become a freestanding starship.

## First-slice playable systems

At briefing the captain chooses no room or one of three pocket rooms. A room consumes one supply on launch and a specialty match boosts chance by 10 percentage points (bounded 5–94). Launch spends one main shield charge. The force shield **never** lets people die in vacuum: at zero main charge a backup safety bubble remains operational but reduces mission success by 10 percentage points. A recharge costs one supply, maximum three. Mission forecast and resolved odds use exactly the same value. New properties are optional for old saves, and the existing store key remains unchanged.

## Comedic and ethical texture

Space Quest / Red Dwarf / Hitchhiker's comic timing, sincere high-stakes intervention, six captain styles, three appointed crewmates, away-team missions, varied villains, Barkeep Vox, the sentient Serendipity bar, and Captain Fluke. The couch generates adventure-scale practical problems: docking etiquette, a passenger who steals half the seats, warp turbulence scattering cushions, a holodeck that materializes behind a guest, and the difference between keeping people breathing and actually protecting them.
