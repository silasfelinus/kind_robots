  title: Slime City · five-alley card battler
  body: >-
    Pick a crew on HOME, then choose a tab. VS is a quick match against the
    CPU or a friend. JOURNEY is your crew's five-chapter story and the
    seven-district city. TOWER is a twelve-floor climb with a two-crew deck.
    COLLECTION is where you build 20-card decks. Every fight is CLASH: five
    alleys, your fighters on the near side, the rival's on the far side. You
    draw five cards a turn and make two plays (one on the opening turn; a
    2 PLAYS card takes both). Tap a card, then a glowing alley, or drag the
    card there. UNDO takes back your last play. FIGHT sends your crew in:
    each fighter strikes the rival across its alley, or the rival boss if the
    alley is open. Bosses start at 40 life; the first to fall loses. Every
    time you play a card it levels up for the rest of the match, so it comes
    back stronger the next time you draw it, up to level three, with a new
    picture. Each crew has one power per match on its boss panel. It works with mouse, touch, keyboard (Tab and
    Enter) or a gamepad. Rewards on your account are still in preview, so
    LOCAL PLAY keeps your journey on this device.
