# Ecotone: chemistry and the four philosophy needles

**Direction, October 20, 2026 PT:** Silas clarified that chemicals should **influence the philosophical options players roll for at the finale**, rather than merely changing prose. This is a newer, narrow change to the October 19 exploratory design in Conductor's `IDEOLOGICAL-ECOTONES.md`: the four axes remain authoritative, but a substance choice now supplies **a modest, reversible, cumulative fictional weighting**. It is neither a claim about real people nor a verdict that one worldview is right.

## Four values already established by the story

1. **Autonomy ↔ Solidarity:** individual choice and privacy versus mutual obligations.
2. **Continuity ↔ Reinvention:** safeguarding what survives versus making something new.
3. **Ecology ↔ Engineering:** living systems versus designed systems.
4. **Agency ↔ Fate:** changing a possible future versus recognizing hard constraints.

These are **questions**, not alignments. A restorative habitat project can involve engineering. A self-reliant character can be generous. An accurate prediction does not invalidate choice.

## Authored chemical nudges

These are literary emphasis weights, not medical effects, personality diagnoses or stereotypes. The visual needle moves toward the named pole when that perspective appears in play:

| Selected experience           | Thematic direction it emphasizes                                    |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Alcohol                       | Solidarity, the social question of belonging and obligation         |
| Cannabis                      | Ecology, interconnected living systems                              |
| Coffee                        | Engineering and Agency, deadlines and control                       |
| Psychedelic fiction           | Reinvention and Fate, unusual futures and the possibility of change |
| Cocaine fiction               | Autonomy and Reinvention, urgency and personal initiative           |
| Meth fiction                  | Engineering and Agency, the pressure of keeping systems working     |
| MDMA fiction                  | Solidarity and Agency, care, consent and accountable action         |
| Water, air, a voluntary pause | Agency; deliberate reconsideration without moral superiority        |

No substance _guarantees_ an ideology. `sober` as a first lens is neutral, not a philosophical advantage; only later intentional pauses create their own agency weight. The opening lens still determines the collectible ID and the opening-signature memory.

### Repetition, mixing and temperance

- A **first** appearance shifts its mapped axis by 1; after **three or more** recorded appearances of the same substance, the shift becomes 2. Further excess does not generate more points. This distinguishes experiments from habits without rewarding binge drinking.
- Two selections in the same scene contribute separately; conflicting pulls can cancel. **Coffee plus cannabis**, the hippie speedball, puts opposing pressure on Ecology/Engineering, with the coffee's separate Agency question still audible.
- Two separate voluntary breaks can nudge the Agency question by up to 2 without removing the recorded chemical history. This is a change of trajectory, not a purity score.
- Chemical pressure is capped at ±2 per axis. Concrete earned decisions contribute up to ±3. The final die modifier is capped at ±4, so **neither substances nor deeds guarantee either philosophical pole**.
- Only real earned flags count. In the broad regional deck, helping with a case (`_care`) raises Solidarity while successful verification (`_verified`) emphasizes Agency; select authored flags such as public workshops, protected land access, consent agreements and collective credit receive more specific weights. Failed rolls are not retroactively awarded an unearned philosophy point.

## The last roll

At the finale, Ecotone rolls **four independent seeded 2d6 results**. Each score is `2d6 + chemical pull + deed pull`, subject to the cap. Less than 7 leans to the first pole, more than 7 to the second, and exactly 7 retains the tension. The Journal shows the _pre-roll needle_ as the run unfolds. The ending shows the dice, the contributions and one conditional authored reflection per axis.

The existing **84 stable endings** remain `7 opening memories × 6 final leads × 2 earned fates`. The four philosophy rolls give emotional coloration **within** those collectible endings, not 84 multiplied by 16 political variants. Hope/dark is still earned by civic consequences, evidence, nerve and, on Wild Way, animal-first choices. Do not make philosophical orthodoxy, ethnicity, sexuality, tribal rights or a chemical an automatic eligibility gate.

## Synthetic acceptance personas

- One-drink learner versus three-occasion drinker versus eight-drink excess: first tug, stronger repeated tug, then a firm cap.
- Same-day overindulgent narrator: existing garbled speech remains; it does not become a superior philosophical or Psyche score.
- Daily drinker: a repeated pattern, not a simulation of acute intoxication.
- Cannabis with coffee: opposing ecosystem versus engineered-world pulls cancel without erasing other dimensions.
- Changed-course temperance: later intentional breaks can rebalance Agency without deleting the initial identity or trapping the player in a moral condemnation.
- Community helper versus private closure: actual flags can move the needle in opposite directions, independent of chemical starting point.
- Every individual substance lens can still roll opposing values on the axis it emphasizes and retains hope/dark access for each companion.

**Gates:** Automated seeds establish consistency and reachability, not authentic reading pace, subjective approval, or community history authorization. No art jobs, schema migration, publication or production deployment in this follow-up.
