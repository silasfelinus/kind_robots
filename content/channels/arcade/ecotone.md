  title: Welcome to Humboldt
  body: |-
    A comedy-horror card adventure through Humboldt County, in the spirit of the strangest X-Files episodes and Twin Peaks' affectionate small-town dread.

    Getting started
    Pick who you are (each background spreads six points across Rizz, Burly, Wits, Local Lore and Psyche), then which white rabbit to chase: the Green Ribbon Fair, an invented cannabis harvest fair at the forest edge, or the Kinetic Grand Championship, the real three-day race of pedal-powered art sculptures, with an invented afterparty.

    Who you meet
    At the fair, the acrobats on the lawn lead to Kit and the drum circle in the woods leads to Juniper. At the race, pit row leads to Cedar and the tattoo table leads to Mara. Then someone offers you something to share: staying sober is always a choice, the fair offers a smoke or a psychedelic brownie, and the race afterparty offers a drink (its two party roads are still being written). It is a voluntary, adult, fictional choice, and it colors the whole run. Then travel with the person you found, or go it alone; going alone has endings of its own.

    The days after
    On the race route a pilot gets hurt at the afterparty and you are conscripted onto the Banana Slug Express for the bay crossing and the Ferndale finish. Each later day opens with two or three face-up cards: pick the corner of the county you want to chase. Later another of the four crosses your path; stay with your guide or follow the newcomer.

    Dice
    Some choices roll two dice plus a skill against 7, 9 or 11. The chip on each choice shows the target and your odds before you commit. Clarity changes a few checks: a clear head helps Wits, a wide-open one helps Psyche. Your guide adds +1 to the skill they're good at. Boxcars always succeed, snake eyes never do, and every failure still moves the story. Rolls are seeded and saved, so reloading the page won't reroll them.

    Endings
    At the end you see the Humboldt of your future: who you finished with (or nobody), the lens you opened with, and whether your goodwill, evidence and nerve earned the hopeful future or the dark one. Every ending you earn is kept in the 70-slot gallery. These finales are first drafts; longer epilogues and more of the county are being written.

Ecotone (working title for Weird Humboldt) is a work-in-progress card adventure. This Play
page carries two opening events, the four companions and the loner road, the Kinetic race
route, daily white-rabbit card choices with seeded 2d6 checks, a stay-or-follow crossroads,
the six-region county map, a journal and the Endings gallery.
