## Seven opening lenses, six focal encounters

The revised scenes **WH-005, WH-028, WH-047, WH-065, WH-086 and WH-107** now each have seven originally authored narrator observations:

- Sober reads concrete contradictions and material risks without gaining privileged access to truth.
- Tipsy notices social comedy and uncertainty, without a moral penalty or compulsory sociability.
- Stoned attends to connections and texture while resisting an unverified revelation.
- Tripping can find momentary absurdist wonder without turning description into a prophetic fact.
- Cocaine notices competing urgency and the possibility of choosing one grounded action.
- Meth allows rest, corroboration and human dignity instead of caricature or supernatural drug powers.
- MDMA keeps warmth distinct from entitlement to other people's time or intimacy.

These **42 asides** leave objective facts, checks, guide decisions and ending access unchanged. They supplement, rather than imitate, the broad creative influences named by Silas: dry speculative humor, humane irony, unexpected civic detail and restrained surrealism.

## Quality and publication gates

The game runs these scenes from `utils/ecotone/regionalData/chapter0*.json`, with a new `regionalLensVoices.ts` for the six asides. The chapter reading kits are mirrors for editors, not alternate active scene sources. Automated tests assert exact 30-scene text parity and seven distinct asides per focal scene.

The 90 other regional stories, full cast voice continuity, cross-scene repetition, culturally sensitive historical fact review, alternate companion family endings and human-paced 45–75-minute play sessions are **not** marked finished. Wiyot/Tuluwat player-facing historical passages still require living-community consultation before publication. No external deployment, generated artwork or additional ArtJobs were authorized by this text-only revision.

**Editorial principle:** A funny scene should remain recognizably human after the strange thing is removed. The failure should still leave the player with a new decision or something worth remembering.
