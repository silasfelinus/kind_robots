# Rainbow Bullet Hell Space Princess: story critic, round 2

Reviewer: independent story/route critic (blind, fresh session)
Build reviewed: `main` at `459343a2`
Lens: master issue #4146 §2 (route map, saves, dialogue beats) and §5 (story, routes, endings)
Scope: read-only. No game code, tests or content changed. The only file this pass adds is this report.

## Verdict up front

Round 1 (`projects-review/rainbow-story-critic.md`, build `4897c0c9`) found 2 blockers and 9 majors. Both
blockers and most majors are fixed on current main, and I checked that by flying the game, not by reading the
fixes:

- Absent pilots no longer speak. Every detour line goes to a pilot who is actually flying.
- Road epilogues play. A loyal ending now reads as a loyal ending from start to finish.
- The Crown's plea is a written scene.
- Lilt-7 acts on both roads. On the rebel road Lilt spends a memory to open the clamps. On the loyal road Lilt
  closes Command's channel.
- The final choice changes the escape, the world line and Lilt's resolution.
- The 15 crew exchanges are now three-line conversations that grow out of who each pilot is.
- Each pilot has her own arc line in every Act.

What's left is one continuity error in the credits, a cluster of epilogue-voicing problems, and polish. I found
**0 blockers, 2 majors and 8 minors**.

## What I checked and found sound

- **Canon.** No dogs and no King Rufus anywhere in the comms of 9 full flights or in the resolver sweep. The
  four princesses, Lilt-7, the Act V memorial reveal (`So every creature we pacified was defending its home,
and we cleared the road for the colonizers.`), the flown fork, and restore/evacuate/sever plus stand-down
  all appear as specified.
- **The fork has playable weight.** The rebel road swaps the B09 fight to the flagship (r1, r3, r14). The loyal
  road keeps the pleading Crown (r8, r7). A loyal crew that flies high during the plea skips the rest of the
  fight and opens the choice on a distinct "turned" ending (r5 from a 2–2 tie, r12 from a trio). Act VI–VIII
  openings differ by road: `Orders stand. We finish this for the Motherland…` vs `Motherland drones are in the
reflections too.`
- **Six named road endings are distinct.** Each has its own world line, Lilt resolution, plate and (loyal) send-off.
- **Save/resume.** A save taken at the Act VII checkpoint and loaded into a fresh engine resumed at S07 with
  `allegiance=rebel`, roster `[2,1]` and all five flags. The finale played the rebel flagship road and the
  correct credits (r15).
- **The 15 reveal exchanges read well.** They're short, in character and answer Lilt directly. Strongest: mask 5
  (`You asked me to fly with you. Start there, Captain.`), mask 12 (Solenne's secret music) and mask 7 (captain
  / map / radio).
- **Act aftermaths and the A06.AFTER exchange** (`You opened that nursery yourself, Lilt.` / `I asked them
first. That is the rule now.`) are the best character writing in the game.

## Verdict

**Ready for human playtesting: yes.** Both round-1 blockers are fixed and verified in flight: only the crew
speaks, and loyal and rebel endings now say different, coherent things. The choice has real consequences in play
and in the endings. The two majors are the stood-down credits naming a flagship that never appeared and the
closing epilogue's voicing; both are small, contained fixes. Ship them before the playtest if possible, but
they don't block it.
