    The water has teeth. Zuzu: The Last Bell is a haunted frontier pinball
    table featuring the River Croc and the Abbess's bell. This is a playable
    public preview, not the final FX3-quality release.
    HOW TO PLAY: Press Start or Enter. Left and right arrows operate the
    flippers. Hold Down to charge the plunger, then release to launch. Up
    nudges the table; repeated nudges cause a tilt. Hold B (Shift) to use
    only the upper Abbey flippers. Gamepads and touch controls also work.
    TABLE RULES: Ring the Bell lane to open the Mortuary and play five
    story chapters. Break the Abbey seals for multipliers; strike the
    captive relic ball to open the hidden undercrypt. The River Croc
    swallows and safely returns the ball; five swallows light an extra ball.
    During Homestead, the Croc opens the Watering Hole, where an
    orbit-to-mouth combo earns a Death Roll jackpot. Three locked Citadel
    balls start Harvest Moon multiball; the broken seals and orbits
    lead to Storm Crow, and five completed story seals start The Last Bell.
    The Bell spiral feeds the raised Abbey deck and its three hollow bells.
    A soft plunge into the Mortuary is the skill shot.
    WORKSHOP NOTES: Scoring and public leaderboards are intentionally
    disabled while this table is in development. Administrators can open the separate Pinball Workshop for
    light-map editing tools.

The Last Bell is a new Zuzu pinball table. This table remains independent
of the main Arcade and from AMI Village Rescue. The full game design,
including mechanical shot routes and the River Croc animated ball-capture
toy, is documented in Conductor under `projects/zuzu-pinball/`.
