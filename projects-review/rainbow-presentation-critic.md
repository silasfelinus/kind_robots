# Rainbow Bullet Hell Space Princess: blind presentation review

**Reviewer:** an independent, fresh-context critic session (presentation lens: issue #4146 §6–§9)
**Build reviewed:** `main` at `4897c0c9` (2026-11-24). I changed no game code.
**Branch:** `claude/rainbow-review-presentation`

## Verdict

**Not playtest-ready on presentation. 4 blockers, 11 majors, 8 minors.**

The game renders cleanly. In about 190k simulated frames across both looks there were no page errors and no missing art. Every Act, boss phase, comms scene and the finale shows painted art. Fever Dream is a lively, mostly coherent look. The hostile "Shots bold" mode is very readable.

The gate in §6–§7 asks for finished motion and finished layered art, and that is not on main:

- **No enemy, boss, ship or pilot frame animation ships.** `RAINBOW_ENEMY_ATLASES = {}` and `RAINBOW_PILOT_POSES = {}`. Every craft is a still sticker that bobs and tilts in code.
- **The 5 "living" Act interludes are effectively stills.** The other 3 Acts have none.
- **The 3 endings are single still plates** and have no Fever Dream version.
- **Main Acts have one or two painted planes, not four or more.** The upper half of X3, Act VIII and the finale does not scroll at all.

On real-cabinet layout, local co-op on touch has the on-screen pads covering the play area and the 4-pilot HUD covering the boss bar. Phone portrait shrinks the stage to about a quarter of the screen, and the close and fullscreen chips sit on top of the comms portrait and HUD text.

## How I tested (so the numbers can be checked)

- **Real cabinet.** I used `utils/scripts/arcadeCabinetFixture.mjs` with `realArcadeStore: true, serveArt: true`. That is the real `arcade-cabinet.vue` and the real engine, with the real `public/rainbow-princess-*` art served. I wrapped it in a 56px header plus a padded content box, `contained: true`, to stand in for the site shell.
- **Campaign sweep.** I stepped the cabinet's attract-demo engine (`window.__games[].game.update`) through the whole campaign: Act I → VIII, plus X3 and the finale, about 189k ticks. I screenshotted every new Act, comms scene, boss, boss phase, and the first frame with ≥12 hostile shots. That gave 116–118 frames per look, at 1440×900 DPR1, in both Classic and Fever Dream. Two small string patches to the fixture bundle kept the attract loop from leaving the demo: `window.__freeze` skips the attract timer, and `window.__clean` hides the DEMO caption.
- **Real page, DPR 1 and 2.** I ran `nuxt dev` (with no database) and loaded `/play/rainbow-bullet-hell-space-princess` at 360×640, 390×844, 768×1024, 1024×768, 844×390, 1440×900 and 1920×1080. I measured page scroll, the canvas box, controls outside the viewport and controls overlapping the stage. I clicked Fly, then the Pilots, Seas, Hangar and Scrapbook tabs.
- **Co-op.** I seated 1, 2 and 4 players with the cabinet's player switch, resumed a real non-demo run at the Act IV boss from a genuine demo save, and screenshotted live 2- and 4-pilot boss fights (touch at 1024×768, 844×390 and 390×844; keyboard plus pads at 1920×1080).
- **Assets and video.** I made contact sheets of the art files. For video I used frame differencing (mean absolute luma difference between frames) on `opening.mp4` and `interludes/*.mp4`.
- **Parallax.** I captured two frames 12 ticks apart per Act and per look, and found the best horizontal shift per tenth of the screen height.
- **Pause and settings.** I simulated a hidden or blurred page and checked Escape. I compared shots normal, bold, and bold + calm.

Synthetic input is not physical-device evidence. Audio was not judged by ear.

## Checked and found fine

- **No page scrolling or overflow.** Page scroll height equals the viewport and scroll width equals the viewport width at every size measured. On the touch cabinet, `html` and `body` overflow are locked. No page errors at any size or DPR. No missing art requests apart from the fixture-only cabinet marquee image.
- **Art covers the whole campaign in both looks.** Acts I–VIII, X3 and the finale all show painted backdrops. Painted bosses appear for all 9 bosses (3 phases each, plus the two flagship stills) and guardians appear. Every Act has a painted title card, except that the finale reuses Act VIII's (P-07).
- **The two looks match.** Fever Dream doodle twins exist for all bosses, enemies, ships, cut-ins and key art. The Fever Dream sticker treatment is cohesive.
- **No text or logo artifacts** in the boss and ship art I inspected. Ship identities (Petal Wing, Prism Lance, Halo Fin, Comet Crown) stay consistent across looks.
- **Hostile shots are readable on most scenes.** Dark-rimmed pellets read well on the darker backdrops (Acts II, III, IV, VII). **Bold shots** (white ring) is excellent everywhere I checked. **Calm motion** removes the moving rainbow band and keeps collision tells.
- **Opening video.** `opening.mp4`, 15s with AAC audio, has real cuts and motion: mean frame differences reach 20–97 at shot changes. It uses the C.A.T.S. pilots, the cat-ships, Lilt-7 and the title.
- **Pause and focus recovery.** Hiding or blurring the page pauses the run. It stays paused when the page comes back (no surprise resume), and Escape resumes and pauses.
- **Comms and subtitles.** Every story scene shows a speaker label, a portrait, and wrapped subtitle text. A COMMS FULL / ESSENTIAL / STORY switch exists. Choices are subtitled ("CHOOSE TOGETHER · 9").
- **Co-op seating.** The player switch cycles 1 to 4. Resumed runs seat 4 distinct ships with distinct colours, HUD rows and lives (3/3/3/3). With 1 to 4 seats, keyboard plus virtual pads produced no errors.
- **Hangar and Scrapbook render their painted art** at 768 and above, and stay within their own panes.
- **Tablet portrait (768×1024).** The tablet layout is acceptable: a full-width stage with the pad below it and nothing occluded.

Not covered: real devices, physical gamepads, DPR 3, browser zoom, audio mix quality, X1, X2 and X4 flights (the demo took the X3 road), and the loyal-road alternate endings.
