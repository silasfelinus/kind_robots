    Four pilots. One pearlescent galaxy.

    This is the private flight deck, not the public Arcade release. Choose your player count in the standard Arcade cabinet and fly through an early playable Act I encounter sequence. The opening Lilt-7 briefing, four successive enemy drills, stranded SOS station, pre-boss warning, three-phase Pearl Seahorse, and rescue aftermath are in this preview. The full-length Act I/II campaign, polished art/cinematics and online co-op are still being built.

    Flight controls
    Use directional controls to steer and hold A to fire. Hold B for precise, slower movement and focused shots; a fully charged super activates on a fresh B press. B no longer gives a free shield refill. Destroy enemy scouts and collect P (power), S (shield), and star (super charge) modules. The cabinet manages keyboard, gamepad, local player seats, pause and touch controls. Ships are numbered as well as color-coded. Choose up to four local pilots on the cabinet. Pause to change the number of local pilots without restarting the Act; benched ships keep their lives and upgrades when they return. With a full earned charge, Solenne's Halo super can revive one fallen active teammate.

    Read the battlefield
    Pinkwake waves introduce picket scouts, spiral scouts, slower anchor mines and pearl urchins. Shoot hostile weapons while dodging their visible projectiles; graze bullets to build charge. The small central hitbox is shown in focus mode. A green SOS station appears mid-run; fly into it to rescue the crew and earn shield repairs. Before B01 Pearl Seahorse arrives, Lilt warns that it carries a living escape pod. Wait for boss targeting pulses, dodge the spread volleys, and destroy its weapons to liberate the pod. Its three health bands change the attacks.

    Future campaign
    The eight planned Acts cross Pinkwake, Moonjelly Fields, Chromatic Trench, Rosefin Express, Blossom Wreckyard, Crown Reef, Glass Current and Eclipse Palace, followed by the final confrontation. This is a shortened Act I vertical slice, not the promised 10–15 minute fully polished Act I, not playable Act II, and not a public score run. The cleared preview remains in a safe transition while Act II is developed.
