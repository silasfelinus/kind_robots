tutorial:
  title: Playbook Forge
  body: >-
    Plays like a console menu. Arrow keys, a gamepad's D-pad or the mouse move
    focus; left and right change the focused value; Enter or A selects; Esc or B
    goes back; Q and E (or LB and RB) switch pages; F (or Menu) opens the launch
    screen; Y opens Assumptions. New playbook starts from a title, an idea and a
    genre, each genre a starting loadout from the playbook that pioneered it.
    Every row shows what one press either way would change in the forecast
    before you commit it. The forecast counts pictures per category, times art
    styles and reroll overhead, at the measured render time of the chosen
    checkpoint; words per unit; agent sessions; and seeded QA playthroughs. Its
    tier comes from the Playbook Kit budget. Forge the project creates the Kind
    Robots project and a scaffold Todo carrying the blueprint to Conductor.
    Analyze a game counts what each playbook has built straight from its code
    and sets it beside the Conductor plan; send a revised plan and the agents
    take it before any roadmap work. Design notes live in Conductor
    projects/playbook-kit/DESIGN-FORGE.md.
