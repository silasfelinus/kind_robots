# Emperor of Dust: 50-day dialogue continuity register

_2026-11-02 PT · t-062 · machine-assisted editorial triage, not a substitute for scored human playthroughs._

## Scope and methodology

This register covers **every one of the 50 authored days** in the six act source files; it also searches the other four named narrative sources (`actTwoCallbacks.ts`, `talks.ts`, `secretTalks.ts`, and `epilogue.ts`) for quoted flag consumers. A **single-appearance candidate** is a day-prefixed flag found once as a literal in that limited narrative corpus, often when a choice sets it and no later narrative scene reads it. It is **not automatically a dead flag**: engine, save, UI, ending-selection, or dynamic flag consumers outside this corpus may use it, and string construction can evade a literal scan. Do not delete or repurpose these flags without a complete repository check.

Reading the page of source is not equivalent to rating every player-visible branch. Each row below is an indexed *triage entry*; choices, branching variants, quest outcome prose, follower conversations and consequences still need 1–5 editorial scoring and multi-persona seeded plays under `projects/emperor-of-dust/playtest/2026-10-21/PLAYTEST-RUBRIC.md`.

## Remaining editorial work

All five acts require a human-editor-quality pass at the **option / response / quest-result** grain. Flag candidates are especially useful for finding choices whose cost never returns, but prose clarity, voice, repeated beats, actual player availability and earned emotional weight must be checked independently. Prioritize named consequences; preserve canonical identities, save-compatible ids, and existing quest postings. Do not infer final art is delivered from prompt queues.
