  body: >-
    Three independent games share one timer. They do not stop when you
    switch your attention. Choose Easy (five hearts, six mines, two slower
    discs, forgiving CPU), Medium (three hearts, ten mines, three discs),
    Hard (two hearts, fifteen mines, four fast discs, sharper CPU) or
    Catastrophic (one heart, twenty mines, six very fast discs, relentless
    CPU) before starting. The difficulty also controls disc respawn pressure,
    invulnerability and player speed. Play chess on the left by tapping a white
    piece then a legal square; Black answers your moves. Reveal mines in the
    middle by tapping a tile, or flag one with a long press, right-click or
    the flag-mode button. On the right, dodge bouncing discs with WASD or by
    dragging your player on touchscreens. Chess moves, safe reveals and disc
    survival all earn points. Work on all three to earn a large balance bonus
    instead of farming only one game. The small bottom bar shows the shared
    timer, score, hearts and pause/leaderboard icons. Choose a run length and
    color theme on the start screen. Pause via the gear icon or Escape.
    Finished 120-second Standard runs can post scores to distinct global
    all-time and daily leaderboards for each difficulty; Blitz and Marathon
    are unranked practice. Guests use three initials, while signed-in
    players also receive their
    account username on the server. The leaderboard uses the Kind Robots
    arcade's existing score validation and rate limits, not cheat-proof replay
    validation. Choose Original three campaigns on the start screen for
    the previous single-board Sugarbomb Kingdom, Ashen Checkmate and Jackpot
    Apocalypse stories, endings, music and scrapbook.
