    Play the five-land illustrated expedition. Draw or choose a reachable road, pick how you answer each encounter, face each land's major trial, use earned supplies, and read the journal of changing places. Each choice's dice chip reads like "Risky 7": how hard the check is, then what your two dice must show once your skill is added. Tap the chip for the sum. Skills on the character sheet reflect your earned history; saved dice keep the rules of the expedition they were rolled in. Use Homestead workshop to visit the original separate prototype. Travel through five shifting lands on an illustrated board: 48 authored encounters, five major trials, earned rewards and a saved journey you can resume. Every card face carries existing Zuzu artwork until each encounter gets its own painting. The game is available while still under development.
