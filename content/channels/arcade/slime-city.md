      body: >-
        ⚔ is attack, the damage a fighter deals. ♥ is toughness, the damage it

    - key: glossary
      title: Glossary of card and combat terms
      body: |-
        **Alley:** One of the five fighting lanes.
        **Ambush:** A new fighter can strike immediately and gains +1 attack on its first strike.
        **Armor:** Reduces incoming damage. **Shield:** Temporary protection against damage.
        **Arrive:** An effect that happens when the fighter enters the battlefield.
        **Attack / power (⚔):** How much damage a fighter deals.
        **Boss:** Your crew leader. Lose when your boss runs out of life.
        **Bulldoze:** Extra damage after defeating a fighter hits the rival boss. A 5-damage hit on an unarmored fighter with 2 life deals 3 damage to the boss.
        **Cast:** Use a spell for its stated effect.
        **Collect:** Gain 1 attack when defeating a fighter.
        **Evolution:** Playing a card unlocks its next level for the match, up to level 3.
        **Faction power:** A crew's special ability, usable once per match.
        **Leech:** Heal 1 life when this fighter hits.
        **New:** A fighter that just entered play; normally it can block but cannot strike yet.
        **Overcharge:** A stronger faction power when your boss is at half life or less, or you fill all five alleys.
        **Overkill:** Damage left over after a fighter runs out of life. Normally this extra damage is lost.
        **Pierce:** Ignore 1 point of armor.
        **Regen:** Heal 1 life after each round.
        **Snare / stun:** A struck or stunned rival skips a strike.
        **Swarm:** Gain 1 attack when beside an ally.
        **Tap:** Use the stated ability instead of this fighter's strike for the turn.
        **Toughness / life (♥):** Damage a fighter can still take. At zero life it is defeated.
        **Trap:** A hidden card that triggers when its condition is met.
        **Volatile:** Explodes when defeated.
