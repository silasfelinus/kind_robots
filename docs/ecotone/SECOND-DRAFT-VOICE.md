# Ecotone second-draft voice and lens contract

**Direction (Silas, October 20, 2026 PT):** The writing should sound hip without performing hipness. Wry, human, intermittently uncanny and a little philosophical; occasionally beautiful, never purple on command. Creative touchstones include the fabulist playfulness of Tom Robbins, the deadpan civic strangeness of *Welcome to Night Vale*, the ordinary-life moral trap of *The Twilight Zone*, the humane satirical economy of Kurt Vonnegut, and the comic escalation of Christopher Moore. These are **high-level qualities to learn from**, not voices to imitate, stock phrases to borrow, or plots to reproduce.

## Sentence-level standard

- A specific Humboldt detail earns each impossible detail its place: wet socks, a crooked harbor form, a road repair, the smell of a metal shop, an official receipt. Start with something someone could actually touch.
- Stay conversational and sharp. Favor a clean short sentence after a weird long one. A joke is funnier when characters take its consequences seriously.
- Let characters be clever, stubborn, loving, embarrassed or confused. Nobody exists merely to embody an ideology, chemical, ethnicity or punchline.
- A supernatural fact stays the same across lenses. Substance use changes **perception and storytelling**, not ownership of truth, moral worth, a permanent political alignment or the power to force a good/bad future.
- Every comic failure should move the scene: a new witness, embarrassment that becomes friendship, a useful wrong answer, debt, an inconvenient document. Never reward all actions with the same glowing goodwill flag.
- No paragraph must end with a lesson. Some scenes should end on a joke, an awkward pause, a tiny concrete change, or an unanswered question.
- Historical atrocity is not absurdist material. Consult the relevant living community before unapproved player-facing Indigenous historical narratives.

## The seven opening lenses

The **opening** lens remains the permanent narrative memory of the weekend. Where mechanics track current state separately, temporary clarity may vary without rewriting that memory. All seven see the same county and can reach hopeful or bittersweet outcomes.

| Lens | What prose notices | What it avoids |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sober** | Checks, omissions, documents, physical stakes, plain unlikely details; understated skepticism | Automatic authority, emotional coldness or uniquely correct insight |
| **Tipsy** | The ridiculous social pact, misheard jokes, awkward fellowship and whose turn it is to speak | Compulsory friendliness, unsafe drinking, incompetent caricature |
| **Stoned** | Patient texture, overlooked relationships, rhythms and patterns that might mean nothing | Claiming private visions establish public facts |
| **Tripping** | Metaphors that briefly feel literal, unstable scale, bureaucratic cosmic horror, uncanny beauty | Endless adjective storms, false revelations, drug-as-superpower |
| **Cocaine** | Racing deadlines, snappy inventory, too many competing demands, the worth of a deliberate pause | Glamour, greedy stereotypes, speed equaling superiority |
| **Meth** | Exhausted repetitions, the dignity of asking for corroboration or rest, human attentiveness in a noisy night | Stigma, sleeplessness treated as special perception, graphic harm or use guidance |
| **MDMA** | Tenderness, social detail, care with consent and boundaries, belonging without compulsory agreement | Magical agreement, entitlement to intimacy, love-as-cure |

## An authored seven-lens micro-example

*Situation: a buoy files a labor complaint because a chart is misleading.*

- **Sober:** The buoy is off station. The chart is wrong. Both facts require attention, in that order.
- **Tipsy:** Someone suggests buying the buoy a drink. The buoy asks for a laminated contract.
- **Stoned:** For a moment you cannot tell where the marker ends and the sea begins. The shipping lane is not interested in the question.
- **Tripping:** The buoy is a punctuation mark that resigned from the sentence. The ocean will not stop speaking.
- **Cocaine:** There are six waiting boats and a deadline. Being urgent is not the same as being useful.
- **Meth:** You read the chart again. The lines do not settle. A rested skipper reads it with you.
- **MDMA:** The buoy wants to be heard. So do the deckhands. Listening does not require putting anyone in danger.

Each line is optional flavor after an **unchanged** scene body. Do not alter the facts, choices or dice thresholds merely because the line sounds differently confident.

## Second-draft acceptance

For each six-chapter batch, ensure a blend of quick gags, dread, small acts of competence, difficult kindness, and no-clear-winner compromises. Deep scenes get path-specific voice treatment. Mechanical uniqueness means materially different options and downstream records, not just three synonyms for being helpful. Editorial review checks for multiple plausible points of view, route-state honesty, continuity of former companions, and nonrepetitive costs. Outcome art must match the actually selected choice and success/failure, not borrow the image of an unearned branch.

The style guide is itself editable. Revisit it after actual playthroughs, not merely after counting completed cards.
