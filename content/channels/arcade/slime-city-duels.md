tutorial:
  title: How to play
  overview: >-
    The same five-alley CLASH as the CPU game, against a real person one turn
    at a time. Knock the rival boss to zero, or hold more boss life when the
    bell rings after round 24.
  body: >-
    Pick a crew, create a private link or an open duel, and send the invite.
    Take your turn whenever you like; the duel waits for you.
  sections:
    - key: invite
      title: Start or answer a duel
      body: >-
        Choose your crew, then PRIVATE LINK (only someone with the link, or the
        friend you challenge, can join) or OPEN TO ANYONE (listed in VS for any
        signed-in player). Copy the invite and send it. The page wakes up when
        your rival joins. Duels pay no Meat or cards, and an untouched duel
        expires after 30 days.
    - key: plays
      title: Two plays a turn
      image: /slime-city/tutorial/two-plays.webp
      body: >-
        Each turn, make two plays, then press FIGHT. Drag a card onto a
        glowing alley, or tap the card and then the alley. A card marked
        2 PLAYS uses both.
    - key: first
      title: The first crew gets one play
      image: /slime-city/tutorial/first-turn.webp
      body: >-
        Whoever opens the match plays just one card on that first turn. After
        that, both crews get two.
    - key: stats
      title: Attack and toughness
      image: /slime-city/tutorial/attack-toughness.webp
      body: >-
        ⚔ is attack, the damage a fighter deals. ♥ is toughness, the damage it
        can take. On FIGHT, every fighter strikes straight across its alley:
        the fighter facing it, or the rival boss if the alley is empty. Bosses
        start at 40.
    - key: ready
      title: Fighters wait one turn
      image: /slime-city/tutorial/fighters-wait.webp
      body: >-
        A fighter that just landed (⏳ NEW) can block but not act. From its
        second turn it strikes, or uses its TAP power instead. Abilities like
        AMBUSH let it strike right away.
    - key: evolve
      title: Cards evolve
      image: /slime-city/tutorial/evolve.webp
      body: >-
        Every time you play a card, your copy levels up for the match, up to
        LV 3, with stronger stats and new art. Tap a card and use the LV tabs
        to preview what it becomes.
    - key: glossary
      title: Glossary of card and combat terms
      body: |-
        **Alley:** One of the five fighting lanes.
        **Ambush:** A new fighter can strike immediately and gains +1 attack on its first strike.
        **Armor:** Reduces incoming damage. **Shield:** Temporary protection against damage.
        **Arrive:** An effect that happens when the fighter enters the battlefield.
        **Attack / power (⚔):** How much damage a fighter deals.
        **Boss:** Your crew leader. Lose when your boss runs out of life.
        **Bulldoze:** Extra damage after defeating a fighter hits the rival boss. A 5-damage hit on an unarmored fighter with 2 life deals 3 damage to the boss.
        **Cast:** Use a spell for its stated effect.
        **Collect:** Gain 1 attack when defeating a fighter.
        **Evolution:** Playing a card unlocks its next level for the match, up to level 3.
        **Faction power:** A crew's special ability, usable once per match.
        **Leech:** Heal 1 life when this fighter hits.
        **New:** A fighter that just entered play; normally it can block but cannot strike yet.
        **Overcharge:** A stronger faction power when your boss is at half life or less, or you fill all five alleys.
        **Overkill:** Damage left over after a fighter runs out of life. Normally this extra damage is lost.
        **Pierce:** Ignore 1 point of armor.
        **Regen:** Heal 1 life after each round.
        **Snare / stun:** A struck or stunned rival skips a strike.
        **Swarm:** Gain 1 attack when beside an ally.
        **Tap:** Use the stated ability instead of this fighter's strike for the turn.
        **Toughness / life (♥):** Damage a fighter can still take. At zero life it is defeated.
        **Trap:** A hidden card that triggers when its condition is met.
        **Volatile:** Explodes when defeated.
