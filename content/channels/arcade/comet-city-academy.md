    Start by choosing an origin (freak accident, mutant, tech, magic,
    alien/dimensional or psychic) and an affinity (fire, air, earth, water,
    discipline, genius, birthright or social). Each of the 48 combinations is a
    different person with a student, hero and villain persona, each with its own
    focus and stats. Your student's strongest stat boosts matching night calls.
    Whether you become the hero or the villain comes from your decisions. Each
    school day, assign volunteers to the
    emergency-response simulation. Each case can take one to three classmates,
    and students cannot work two assignments at once. Matching your responder's
    skill to a mission improves the result, but everyone has different strengths.
    Unassigned cases still affect your record. After training, draw a campus
    encounter and choose whom to trust. Your decisions affect influence and danger.
    Complete seven days to face the Comet Ball finale and record an ending.
    The game auto-saves locally after each decision. This is the first playable
    chapter, not the complete planned twenty-one-day adventure.
