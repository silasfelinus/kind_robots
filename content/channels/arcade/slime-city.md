tutorial:
  title: Slime City · five-alley card battler
  overview: >-
    Six rival crews fight for a radioactive megacity, and every fight is
    CLASH: five alleys, two plays a turn, and cards that evolve each time you
    play them. Knock the rival boss to zero, or hold more boss life when the
    bell rings after round 24.
  body: >-
    Pick a crew on HOME, then choose a tab. VS is a quick match against the
    CPU or a friend. JOURNEY is your crew's five-chapter story and the
    seven-district city. TOWER is a twelve-floor climb with a two-crew deck.
    COLLECTION is where you build 20-card decks. Rewards on your account are
    still in preview, so LOCAL PLAY keeps your journey on this device.
  sections:
    - key: modes
      title: Pick a crew and a mode
      body: >-
        HOME picks your crew. VS is a quick match against the CPU, or a duel
        against a friend in the Two-Player Pit. JOURNEY follows your crew
        through a five-chapter story across the seven-district city. TOWER is
        a twelve-floor climb with a two-crew ALLIANCE deck. COLLECTION builds
        20-card decks from 300 cards.
    - key: board
      title: Five alleys, two rows
      body: >-
        Your fighters hold the near row of five alleys; the rival's hold the
        far row. Every fighter strikes straight across its alley: it hits the
        fighter facing it, or the rival boss if that alley is empty. Bosses
        start at 40 life.
    - key: plays
      title: Two plays, then FIGHT
      body: >-
        You draw five cards a turn and make up to two plays; the glowing play
        icons show how many are left. A card marked 2 PLAYS takes both, and
        the crew that opens the match gets one play on its first turn. Drag a
        card onto a glowing alley, or tap the card and then the alley. UNDO
        takes back your last play against the CPU. FIGHT sends every fighter
        in, alley by alley, and ends your turn.
    - key: cards
      title: Where cards go
      body: >-
        Fighters land in your own alleys (landing on your own fighter replaces
        it). Traps hide in your alley and spring when a rival fighter lands
        across from them or strikes through with nothing to block it. Gear and
        buffs go on your fighters; spells and strikes hit a rival fighter.
        Only the alleys that glow are legal for the card you hold.
    - key: status
      title: Read the fighter tags
      body: >-
        NEW (⏳) fighters just landed: they block but cannot strike until your
        next turn, unless they have AMBUSH. TAP (↷) marks a fighter with an
        ability: select it and press TAP to use the ability instead of its
        strike. 🛡 is armor, WALL is a shield for the next hit, STUN skips a
        strike.
    - key: evolve
      title: Cards evolve as you play them
      body: >-
        Every time you play a card, your copy levels up for the rest of the
        match, up to level three, with stronger stats and a new picture. Tap
        any card in your hand or any fighter on the board to see it full size,
        and use the LV 1, LV 2 and LV 3 tabs to preview its evolutions. Some
        level threes let you choose between two forms.
    - key: power
      title: One faction power per match
      body: >-
        Each crew has one power on its boss panel. It does not use a play,
        works once per match during your play phase, and hits harder when
        OVERCHARGED: your boss at half life or less, or a fighter in every one
        of your five alleys. ALLIANCE decks bring both crews' powers.
    - key: controls
      title: Controls
      body: >-
        The deck icon opens and closes your hand, so it floats over the board
        instead of taking space. Slime City works with mouse, touch, keyboard
        (Tab and Enter) or a gamepad.
