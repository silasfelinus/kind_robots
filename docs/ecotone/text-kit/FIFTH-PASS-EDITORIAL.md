## Four guide-specific conversations

At WH-008, WH-023, WH-065 and WH-082, each of Mara, Kit, Juniper and Cedar has a distinct point of view delivered through `leadLines`, but **only if actively traveling with the protagonist**. A solo run never fabricates the presence of a companion. These sixteen conditional lines are independent of the seven opening lenses.

- **Mara:** consent, professional autonomy and dignity even when business is precarious.
- **Kit:** theatrical craft, paid work, rehearsal safety and privacy beyond the stage.
- **Juniper:** unglamorous care, boundaries, and the distinction between protecting life and turning it into spectacle.
- **Cedar:** accountable engineering, sober risk assessment, and the learned solidarity of his logger father and activist mother, without inventing other historical connections.
