# Ecotone second draft, prose redline: round 1

**Editorial status:** Working manuscript, not final canon. October 20, 2026 PT. These are twelve selected playable scenes, two per regional chapter, not a claim that the remaining 108 have received the same depth of treatment. The canonical game JSON and corresponding chapter reading-kit documents now agree.

## The tonal rule

Humboldt first; weirdness second; a person who needs something always wins the paragraph over a clever metaphor. Stay conversational, funny in a dry sideways manner, capable of surprise or sadness without a compulsory moral lesson. The speculative humor, humane satire, civic oddity and gentle dread requested by Silas are general creative qualities, not imitation of any single living writer's style. The permanent opening lens still colors interpretation; it does not establish facts, worthiness, ideology or access to a happy future.
