# Rainbow Bullet Hell Space Princess: blind story and route critique

Reviewer: independent story/route critic (fresh session, no prior context)
Build reviewed: `main` at `4897c0c9`
Lens: issue #4146 §5 (story, routes, endings, character moments) and §9 (false choices, continuity, reachability)
Scope: read-only. Nothing in the game was changed.

## Verdict

**Not story-ready.** The routing works. Every advertised road, fork, detour and named ending can be reached from
real input. A tied vote stays loyal, and a saved road survives a reload. The rebel road also really changes
Acts VI–IX: truce schools, Motherland drones and the flagship fight. The trouble is the words around it. Most
playthroughs show at least one of these problems:

1. **Pilots who are not in the crew speak on comms.** Every detour (X1–X4) is a fixed four-pilot script. A solo
   Vivi run hears Maribel, Azure and Solenne talking. This happened in every non-quad run that took a detour.
2. **The road epilogues are dead code.** The engine always passes a party mask, so the six authored rebel/loyal
   epilogues never play. A road-blind party line replaces them, and it directly contradicts the loyal endings.
   For example, _"PEACE BY ORDER … The reef goes quiet."_ is followed by _"The distress channel belongs to
   everyone now"_ and then _"The Motherland expects us home."_
3. **The final choice changes one line, a title and a painting.** The escape, Lilt's resolution, the crew
   epilogue, the verdict and (except loyal‑sever) the send-off are the same for restore, evacuate and sever.
4. **The late course-correction has no voice.** The Crown "pleads", but the plea is a HUD countdown
   (`THE CROWN PLEADS · FLY HIGH TO STAND DOWN · 7`) with no line from the Crown, Lilt or the crew. A stood-down
   crew's ending never acknowledges that it turned at the last moment.
5. **The 15 pilot-combination lines are single non-sequiturs.** They refer to things the game never sets up: a
   "starport", an "error chime", a "radio duet", "academy records", a "transmitter". Vivi delivers 8 of the 15.
6. **The princesses are interchangeable.** The Act dialogue is the same for every pilot with the speaker name
   swapped. In 3- and 4-pilot crews, the middle seats speak in about 1–5 scenes of roughly 80. Character-specific
   lines are re-assigned by seat, which hands one pilot's role to another.
7. **Lilt-7 has no agency beyond refusing to choose.** Every run says some form of "I will not choose for you"
   four times. Lilt never acts, disagrees, takes a risk or changes an outcome.

I count 2 blockers, 9 majors and 11 minors (table below).

## How I tested

- I wrote a headless driver: `projects-review/rainbow-story-critic/fly.ts` (run with `npx tsx`). It creates the
  real engine with `demo: false` and `selectScreen: true`, picks the roster on the real pilot-select screen
  (Easy difficulty), and then flies with ordinary held/pressed buttons only. There is no demo immortality and no
  snapshot editing. It holds fire, weaves, and steers to the lane the policy wants at each fork, the memorial,
  the Navigator, the Crown's plea, the finale lanes and the ▲/▼ detour signposts. When the crew dies, it presses
  A on CONTINUE?. Every 8 frames it renders through a stub 2D context and records each new `fillText` string in
  order, which gives a transcript of everything the player could read.
- To reproduce a run, use `OUT=<dir> npx tsx projects-review/rainbow-story-critic/fly.ts '<policy JSON>'` with
  the policy from the table below. Seeds are fixed and the engine is deterministic.
- 18 runs. All 15 non-empty pilot masks, crews of 1–4, all six road×choice endings, a stand-down from each road

## What I checked and found fine

- **Reachability from real input.** All six named road endings (ASK FIRST, OPEN WATER, NO MORE LEASHES, PEACE BY
  ORDER, AN EMPTY OCEAN, ORDERS BROKEN) were reached by flown lanes, not hand-set flags. So were the stand-down
  (from loyal 4-pilot and 3-pilot crews), both sides of the trench/wreck/reef forks, Navigator meet/skip, and
  detours X1 (fly low at the dock with Nemi), X2, X3 and X4. Each detour is signposted on screen (`▲ X2 CORAL
COMET / ▼ ACT V WRECKYARD`, etc.). The failure ending ("THE ECLIPSE HOLDS" plus three Lilt lines) closes the
  run correctly.
- **Vote rules.** A 2–2 and a 1–1 memorial split stayed loyal. A 2–1 split went rebel. A 3-way finale split went to
  the lead's lane, as documented in `allegianceFor` and `finaleChoiceFor`.
- **Save/continue keeps the road.** A save taken at Act VII and reloaded into a fresh game resumed with
  `allegiance=rebel` and roster `[2,1]` intact (r15).
- **The rebel/loyal choice has playable consequences.** The rebel road changes Acts VI–VIII waves (truce schools
  plus Motherland drones, `rainbowRebelRoad.ts`) and swaps the B09 fight (flagship with clamp lanes vs pleading
  Crown). Act VI–VIII openings and Act VII/VIII bridge lines change by road and read well. "Orders stand. We
  finish this for the Motherland, whatever it is protecting." is a good loyal line.
- **The Act I–V evidence build-up is legible and paced.** Motherland sigils in the trench, a collar "stamped with
  old fleet command", colony ships booked on the rail, depot crews who "were not its prisoners. They were its
  caretakers". The memorial reveal lands ("So every creature we pacified was defending its home, and we cleared
  the road for the colonizers."), and the ▲/▼ lane choice that follows is clear.
- **Rescue callbacks exist and fire from real flags.** In Act VIII the Courier, Navigator, Nemi, depot survivors
  and the relay answer as allies (`ALLIES ANSWER ×n`, `A08.TURN / COURIER — "Their route codes match the convoy
you saved. They are crews, not cargo."`). The credits name exactly the rescues made, and route lines appear
  only for detours flown.
- **Boss aftermaths are specific and humane.** Each Act ends with an `.AFTER` scene that names the boss's fate
  ("Clawburst is down and its pilot is alive"; "The Glass Eel's mirrored shell is peeling back. It is free.").
- **Ending pacing.** Ending lines hold 3 s each and A advances them after a 0.5 s grace. Credits can be skipped and
  THE END waits for A. None of the dialogue strings in `rainbow*.ts` overflows the two-line comms box at the
  57-column portrait wrap. I checked this mechanically.
- **The pilot-select roster is honoured.** HUD tags (`P1 VIVI + GLITCH`), credits crew list and call sign follow
  the chosen pilots, and the 15 party masks key on pilot identity rather than seat.

## Not covered by this pass

- Real-cabinet rendering (portraits, the opening video, whether the speaker tag is legible at phone width). The
  transcript comes from the engine's own `fillText` calls, not from screenshots.
- Unsworn (pre-memorial legacy save) endings, which only legacy saves can reach.
- Difficulty, fairness and run length. My bot needed 9–36 continues and 45–80 minutes per clear on Easy, which
  says more about the bot than the game.
