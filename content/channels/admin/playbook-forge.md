tutorial:
  title: Playbook Forge
  body: >-
    Plays like a console menu. Arrow keys, a gamepad's D-pad or the mouse move
    focus; left and right change the focused value; Enter or A selects; Esc or B
    goes back; Q and E (or LB and RB) switch pages; F (or Menu) opens the launch
    screen; Y opens Assumptions. New playbook starts from a title, an idea and a
    genre, each genre a starting loadout from the playbook that pioneered it.
    Every row shows what one press either way would change in the forecast
    before you commit it. The forecast counts pictures per category, times art
    styles and reroll overhead, at the measured render time of the chosen
    checkpoint; words per unit; agent sessions; and seeded QA playthroughs. Its
    tier comes from the Playbook Kit budget. Forge the project creates the Kind
    Robots project and a scaffold Todo carrying the blueprint to Conductor.
    Load a game opens its production ledger: every beat its Playbook Studio
    adapter knows, grouped into sections, with art delivered, rendered but not
    wired, queued, failed or missing, scene and outcome text, the relay time
    its ArtJobs took and the render time and words still left. A finished
    section earns a star; every section starred with the plan met is super
    shiny. Discrepancies are listed. Submit art sends a section's unpainted
    beats to kr-relay (at most 60 at a time); Send writing gives its missing
    words to the next agent as a Todo. Y (or Beats) opens Playbook Studio for
    that game: every beat with its scene editor, overlays that go live, notes,
    flags and art requests. F (or Menu) opens the game's plan:
    built against planned, and a revised plan to send. Kit console on the title
    screen rehearses games built on the Playbook Kit. Design notes live in
    Conductor projects/playbook-kit/DESIGN-FORGE.md.
