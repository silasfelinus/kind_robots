  title: Art Slots
  body: >-
    An art slot is one place an image is used: a Slime City card, an evolution
    stage, a Ruler Hooked portrait. Conductor adds every finished render for a
    slot as a candidate, so redos and re-renders sit side by side instead of
    getting lost. Filter by project or by the start of the slot key, and keep
    "Only slots with a choice to make" on to see slots with two or more
    candidates. Use marks the candidate the slot should show; the heart and the
    cross record likes and rejections for whoever curates next. Find
    duplicates lists renders with the same prompt, seed and model, which are
    the same picture; moving them to the Recycle Bin keeps the image in use
    (or else the oldest copy) and can be undone from the Recycle Bin.
