    A shared archive of Zuzu art across every production. Search, or filter by
    project, media type and production role, and click an image to see its
    source project, format, and how each project uses it. Use in project links
    the image to another production without copying it, as a general
    reference, shot keyframe, character design, stage background or animation
    clip, with an optional scene or slot label and pinned version; the pencil
    on a usage edits its role and the cross unlinks it, never deleting the
    image. Usage chips on a card jump to that project's art. Repository media
    (git) lists the gamebook plates, Showdown sprites, portraits and stages,
    and Ghost Trail plates that ship as files, with their manifest, whether the
    file is on this server, and a link back to the ArtImage each was exported
    from. Request edit files a private agent task describing a visual change.
    The buttons along the top open Canon & lore, characters, story scenarios,
    props, LoRAs, the Comic Studio, music videos, and the gamebook.
