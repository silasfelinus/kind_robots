# Punk Bloodfairies: full-faction evolution editorial pass

Implements the second faction of Conductor `slime-city/t-041` and the 900-stage campaign, after Catgirls. **40/40 individual card reviews covering 120/120 L1/L2/L3 stage roles**. Ten founder/legacy cards retain their hand-built Clash handlers but receive fresh standalone three-stage rules text. All 30 data-authored cards receive new, individually written L1 stage roles; 13 of their arcs gain specific new mechanics. No inventory changes, no art claims, no economy edits.

The objective is a distinct Bloodfairies play style: fragile concert crowds, SWARM adjacency, LEECH and earned encore beats. An ability that can never fire is not an evolution; spell stages can only cast on arrival, traps only spring, and combat/tap triggers belong to living fighters or their installed gear.
