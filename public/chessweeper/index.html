Two ways to win

Safe cells uncovered0 / 22

Win by checkmating Black or by revealing every safe tile in the mined middle four ranks. Minesweeper and chess share the same board.

  1. Chess: all standard moves, including promotion and castling.
  2. Mines: hidden in rows 3–6, indicated by clues. Landing on one explodes your piece.
  3. Discs: bounce one square after every move or probe, shredding pieces and chipping king armor.
  4. Probing: costs your chess turn, so Black gets to move.
