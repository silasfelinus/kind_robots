tutorial:
  title: Playbook Studio
  body: >-
    Pick a story in the left rail. The Story Atlas shows every beat as an
    art card with its kind, place and status (ready, needs art, art
    requested, draft or live edit, ending). Collection narrows it to a route
    or a section such as the endings or Emperor of Dust's follower and quest
    lines; Refine filters by kind, day, who a beat is written for and its
    variations. Chain shows the same beats as chains of choices, each naming
    where it leads. Open a card to edit the scene: its art sits on the left
    with a line from the beat and its art candidates, the Edit Scene panel on
    the right. Every game's text can be edited (Ecotone's whole card, the
    others' words); Save draft keeps an edit here, Save and go live puts it
    into the game for every player, and Revert returns the authored text.
    Rolls, effects and links stay as authored. Comments, image flags,
    submitted images and art requests work everywhere, tagged to each game's
    project and art lane. Export downloads a game's edits and open notes as
    JSON for an agent to fold into the source.
