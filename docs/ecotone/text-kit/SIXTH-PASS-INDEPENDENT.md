## Editorial goals for future human playthroughs

Look for strong dialogue without moralizing; a distinct cost for each option; care outcomes that are neither patronizing nor automatically optimal; and consequences that do not reduce every conflict to successful paperwork. Keep lore and fiction separate. These eight scenes do not incorporate Wiyot, Tuluwat, tribal place names, historical testimony or culturally specific designs.

Their existing choice-specific editorial art briefs inherit the new canonical wording. No new ArtJobs are submitted by this writing pass; any separately claimed art-standard project retains its independent work and consultation gates.
