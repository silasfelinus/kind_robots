# Ecotone second-draft editorial: regional completion audit

**Working manuscript, not publication approval.** October 20, 2026 PT.

## Coverage and honest definition of done

The canonical playable deck contains **120 WH-001–WH-120 encounters**. Of these, **111 regional manuscripts** have reading-kit counterparts across six chapters; the other **nine are original hand-authored encounters** in `content.ts` and `questExpansion.ts`.

- Rounds 1–3 reviewed **48 regional scenes**, changing narrative beats, choice language and consequence text; rounds 2–3 also added 84 original, lens-specific observations to twelve focal scenes.
- This final regional pass reviews and edits the other **63 regional scenes**: nine in Arcata, nine on the Coast, ten in Eureka, eleven on the Backroads, twelve in Southern Humboldt and twelve at the Kinetic Festival. Arcata and Coast receive complete six-field scene/choice/outcome rewrites. The remaining chapters receive focused, story-specific rewrites of at least escalation, recoverable failure and concrete care consequence; several receive additional choice and successful investigation rewrites. Previously strong material is deliberately not changed for change's sake.
- The nine legacy encounters were audited individually: WH-003, WH-006, WH-012, WH-021, WH-024, WH-027, WH-043, WH-055 and WH-061. WH-003 and WH-027 received sharpened failure/alternative outcomes; WH-006 a consequential failed rope investigation; WH-021 a more human failed tide follow-up and **all seven opening lenses**, replacing incomplete four-lens coverage; WH-061 a safer rescue-failure outcome and distinction from similarly named WH-046. WH-012, WH-024, WH-043 and WH-055 retain their stronger character-led second-draft dialogue and separately gated companion stakes.
- **120/120 cards have received this editorial audit; 111/111 regional manuscripts have review edits.** This is *not* a claim of 120 new stories, 120 new art assets, final playtest acceptance, historical certification or authorization to publish.

## Specific craft decisions

The smallest visible choice should still matter after the paranormal premise is removed. The failed roll leaves a damaged document, surviving timestamp, corroborating witness, safe detour or genuine complication, not a generic shrug. Care outcomes usually keep somebody safe, paid, credited, listened to or in charge of their own story. Investigation gathers evidence without giving the player authority to override a worker, guide, witness, or family. An apparent historical document is a fictional clue until verified by an appropriate source.

Recurring anti-patterns corrected in this pass:

1. **Proof by atmosphere.** The moon lecturer, two-horizon photograph, memory jars and moving fog now leave checkable records or clearly unverifiable recollections.
2. **Rescue as spectacle.** The plunge, tidepool, harbor, river, dune and kinetic cards prioritize the people in the water or on the road rather than the perfect paranormal photograph.
3. **Care as compulsory happiness.** A shy cryptid keeps their location private, a family can decline an interview, a performer may end a show, a scarecrow can request rest, and a disputed neighbor does not have to publish memories.
4. **Substances as character alignment.** The Moonstone legacy picnic now has all seven voluntary opening observations with equal access to the underlying choices. Distinct lens attention never adds unique evidence, mystical certainty or a required moral outcome.
5. **Character voices as thesis statements.** When Juniper, Kit, Mara and Cedar already speak in specific, consequential terms, those passages stay put. The rest of the deck now gives side characters similarly grounded needs.

## Contracts protected

All **WH IDs, scene/pool routing, seed identity, underlying skill checks and targets, effects, choice IDs, gallery slots, save keys and outcome-art keys** remain intact. Six editor-readable chapter kits now mirror the playable JSON, including each revised card and outcome art brief. The Ecotone contract suite now checks **all 111 regional cards** for playable/manuscript parity, not the previous selected sample of 48, and explicitly protects seven-lens WH-021 coverage.

This was a text-only revision. No ArtJobs were enqueued, no production deployment performed, and no gameplay-system, ending-path or persistent-save migration was introduced.

## Unclosed human/release gates

**Historical/community review:** Wiyot/Tuluwat/1860 history is not an uncanny puzzle, branching reward, or supernatural explanation. This pass did not add player-facing tribal testimony or historical claims. Before a public release containing those passages, arrange living-community consultation and fact-check the exact text and attributions; obtain explicit creative and publication approval. Fictional ancestry or a local place name is not a substitute for consultation.

**Human-paced playtesting:** Automated seeded runs previously demonstrated 84/84 ending reachability and save compatibility but **did not establish the promised 45–75-minute experience**. At least one fresh reader should complete each opening type with a stopwatch (festival, kinetic, quiet), plus a failure-heavy run; record actual time, skipped prose, confusing offers, continuity or safety issues, and where they stopped caring. Retest at phone, tablet and desktop widths with keyboard-only navigation and a screen reader. These are genuine acceptance activities, not passed checks to backfill with a synthetic estimate.

**Editorial acceptance:** Review the 120-card playthrough experience as a connected work, including repeated-choice fatigue, ending callbacks, visual-art fidelity and companion/found-family continuity. The separate optional multi-companion route remains scoped to t-045. Formal launch and art/publication gates remain with Silas.

The point of this pass: an impossible county should still be inhabited by people who need rides, breaks, consent, a fair hearing, and the occasional refund.
