  title: Welcome to the weekend
  body: |-
    A comedy-horror card adventure through Humboldt County, in the spirit of the strangest X-Files episodes and Twin Peaks' affectionate small-town dread.

    Getting started
    Pick who you are this weekend (each background spreads six points across Rizz, Burly, Wits, Local Lore and Psyche), then choose how you meet the festival: Sober, Tipsy, Stoned or Tripping. It is a voluntary, adult, fictional choice, and it decides who you meet first: Mara, Kit, Juniper or Cedar. Travel with them or go alone.

    Dice
    Some choices roll two dice plus a skill against 7, 9 or 11. The chip on each choice shows the target and your odds before you commit. Clarity changes a few checks: a clear head helps Wits, a wide-open one helps Psyche. Your guide adds +1 to the skill they're good at. Boxcars always succeed, snake eyes never do, and every failure still moves the story. Rolls are seeded and saved, so reloading the page won't reroll them.

    Crossroads
    Later in the weekend another of the four crosses your path. Stay with your guide or follow the newcomer; whoever you leave keeps chasing their own piece of the mystery.

    What's here so far
    This Play page is a preview of the first three chapters. Each weekend deals two cards per chapter from a growing deck: the Minor Theatre trapdoor, the Arcata Marsh, Fickle Hill, the Bigfoot picnic, the 1849 argument that named the Mad River, the Perilous Plunge, a tribute to the zoo's lost friends, the Bayshore Mall after hours and the graffiti behind it. Then comes a crossroads, the county map, your journal and the 56-slot Endings gallery. Chapters four to six, the other three opening roads and the earned endings are being written.

Ecotone (working title for Weird Humboldt) is a work-in-progress card adventure. This Play
page carries the prologue, the four companion introductions, a seeded deck of encounters with
seeded 2d6 checks, a stay-or-follow crossroads, the six-region county map, a journal and
the Endings gallery.
