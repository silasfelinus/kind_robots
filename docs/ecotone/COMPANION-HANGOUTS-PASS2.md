# Ecotone: personal companion interludes (t-045, second playable slice)

Four new optional scenes follow a **mutually accepted two-guide invitation** at the existing crossroads. Each lets the invited guide express a substantive need, decline the player's framing, and set boundaries.

| Guide   | Scene                                  | Dilemma                                                                                       |
| ------- | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kit     | The Wheel That Refuses Applause        | Risk a kinetic show for an apprentice, respect a safety veto, or offer unintrusive help       |
| Juniper | A Tree Cannot Sign a Volunteer Sheet   | Balance habitat with accountable drainage and a neighbor's damaged livelihood                 |
| Cedar   | The Radio That Knows Your Father       | Verify an uncanny broadcast without making Cedar's actual family responsible for it           |
| Mara    | The Bird With Someone Else's Signature | Protect an artist's work without treating client privacy or creative ownership as a spectacle |

Each has a challenging checked choice with separate success and failure, plus two substantive unrolled alternatives. The 16 resulting memories each have a unique long-term callback in the **real ending view**. Failing the check doesn't award a disguised victory. Refraining from involvement isn't punished as jealousy or treated as a declaration of romance. Any eventual relationship must be earned.

The cards live in the shared authored encounter catalog and have unique slots for a scene painting and each specific outcome, visible to the Playbook editing/art standards. They are **not** offered in the ordinary seeded fork deck, and they do not submit their planned slots to a generation queue. t-042's art worker owns that job.

Mechanically, the encounter is inserted _after_ the crossroads resolution but _before_ the next route step only when the saved choice was `together`. The route index remains unchanged. Legacy stay/follow/solo routes, fixed endpoints, ongoing saved games, and the original 56 collectible signatures remain intact. The optional scene's outcome still uses the standard dice/effect/journal machinery.

The gameplay regression suite exercises four invited guides with three existing-lead combinations each, over seeded successes/failures, resumed mid-scene saves, continuation to the next route step, every art-slot key, all distinct memory callbacks, and no phantom encounters on declined or switched-guide paths.

**Further development**: other voluntary check-in opportunities for previously met guides, actual long-term bond progression (rather than assuming intimacy), three- and four-guide party opportunities, collectible group ending signatures, and matching four-style art. The held cultural material, real historical claims and public release gates remain unchanged.
