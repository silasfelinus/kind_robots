  title: Deal a story onto the Table
  body: >-
    Storybook opens on the Table. Across the top are an optional Working
    title, a Spark (Objective in Taskmaster mode) and the Length: Short,
    Standard, Long or Epic, the deck default, or Endless for an Open-ended
    story. Tap a slot on the cloth (Mode, Genre, Place, Hero, Company,
    Narrator, Thread or Treasures) and deal a card into it from the hand along
    the bottom; Genre, Place and Hero are required, and a dealt Narrator adds a
    Delivery choice. Open-ended has no last page, Episodic plays a plot thread
    over a set run of scenes, Structured tells one life weighed across ten
    hidden dimensions, and Taskmaster builds a quest from one of your own
    projects, changing nothing real until you press Accept on a proposal. Sign
    in to press Open this story; each narrated turn uses mana. On every page,
    take an offered option, write your own move, or play one of Your cards.
    Chronicle reopens earlier adventures, and an ending offers Play again or
    New table.
