  title: Slime City · five-alley card battler
  overview: >-
    Six crews, five alleys. Knock the rival boss from 40 to zero, or hold
    more boss life when the bell rings after round 24.
  body: >-
    Win by reducing the rival boss to zero health. Both bosses normally start
    at 40. Ready fighters hit the rival boss when their opposing alley is
    empty and no trap stops them. If both bosses survive round 24, the boss
    with more health wins; equal health is a draw.
    Pick a crew on HOME. VS is a quick match, JOURNEY is your crew's story,
    TOWER is a twelve-floor climb, and COLLECTION builds 20-card decks.
  sections:
    - key: victory
      title: How to win
      body: >-
        Clear the opposing alley so your fighters can hit the rival boss.
        For example, a ready fighter with 5 attack in an empty, untrapped
        alley takes 5 health from the rival boss. Keep fighters opposite
        enemy attackers to protect your own boss. Reduce the rival boss to
        zero to win; at the end of round 24, higher boss health wins (equal
        health is a draw).
    - key: plays
      title: Two plays a turn
      image: /slime-city/tutorial/two-plays.webp
      body: >-
        Each turn, make two plays, then press FIGHT. Drag a card onto a
        glowing alley, or tap the card and then the alley. A card marked
        2 PLAYS uses both.
    - key: first
      title: The first crew gets one play
      image: /slime-city/tutorial/first-turn.webp
      body: >-
        Whoever opens the match plays just one card on that first turn. After

    - key: stats
      title: Attack and toughness
      image: /slime-city/tutorial/attack-toughness.webp
      body: >-
        ⚔ is attack, the damage a fighter deals. ♥ is toughness, the damage it
        can take. On FIGHT, every fighter strikes straight across its alley:
        the fighter facing it, or the rival boss if the alley is empty. Bosses
        start at 40.
    - key: ready
      title: Fighters wait one turn
      image: /slime-city/tutorial/fighters-wait.webp
      body: >-
        A fighter that just landed (⏳ NEW) can block but not act. From its
        second turn it strikes, or uses its TAP power instead. Abilities like

    - key: evolve
