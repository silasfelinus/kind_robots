  title: "Tzaddikim: the 36 and the nominations"
  body: >-
    Tzaddikim borrows the folklore of the 36 righteous or just people said to
    sustain the world, then plays with that idea as a sourced gallery of people
    worth celebrating. It is entertainment and public biography, not a Jewish
    religious classification: no one needs to be, or is implied to be, Jewish
    or Hasidic. Browse Living, Memorial and Pop Culture, then use contribution
    tags to narrow each category. Each view presents its highest-scoring
    36 approved nominees first, with all other eligible nominees in a second
    gallery on the same page. The community score counts one effective upvote
    or downvote per voter, plus star-review points above or below three stars.
    Votes can be changed or cleared. Signed-in members vote with the up/down
    controls, open a portrait to write a detailed rating or review, and use
    Submit a candidate to nominate somebody new. Community scores rank the
    gallery; they do not bypass editorial approval or prove moral worth.
    Wikipedia and Wikimedia are the default sources for biographical facts
    and art. Source links and image provenance stay visible, and editor
    overrides must preserve that history. Every biography includes documented
    objections or controversies where meaningful, without inventing false
    balance. Discovery deliberately looks beyond US and UK celebrities across
    countries, languages, disciplines and types of service. Admins can also
    review pending and archived nominations and browse the daily suggestion
    pool. This tutorial is available from the ? workspace sidebar.
