    Thirty-two stages, one determined heroine.

    Save the Plumber is planned as a full eight-world side-scrolling adventure. This Admin page is its front door: the title screen, a prologue, the overworld map, the eight heroines, the cast and the Arcade cabinet, where all eight worlds are playable: thirty-two stages, eight bosses and the rescue finale. The opening cutscene and powerups are forthcoming.

    Movement lab
    Scroll to the cabinet and press Start. Choose New Game, then pick one of eight heroines with the arrows and press A (B picks one at random). Use Left and Right to run, and A or Up to jump; hold A to jump higher. Jump input is buffered just before landing and remains available for a few frames after leaving a ledge. The cabinet saves your heroine and stage on this device, so Continue picks up where you left off. The Arcade cabinet also manages gamepad, touch, pause and sound.

    Tools and enemies
    Press B to splash your wrench ahead: it defeats pumpbugs, stuns rivet rollers, breaks crates and turns valve switches, which open blue water gates. Landing on a pumpbug defeats it; landing on a rivet roller stuns it, and touching a stunned roller kicks it into its friends. Steam vents puff on a beat, springsteps launch you high, and pistons and brass lifts carry you over pits. Press Down while standing on a pipe to enter a secret room. Three hearts protect each life, checkpoint pennants save your place, and turning the goal valve clears a stage. In 1-4, jump Madame Pressure's steam waves and stomp her while she wobbles. In Tideglass Marina, water costs a life, rafts bob on the swell, clockwork minnows leap from the waves, cork crabs scuttle and glass gulls swoop to steal your tokens (stomp one to get them back); Captain Barnaclejack's anchor glows before it sweeps, so jump clear and stomp him while it is stuck. In Lantern Orchard, stomp a moss lantern to flatten it into a step, bounce off spring hares to reach high ledges, and carry your own light through the unlit Rootcellar and Firefly Canopies; the Overripe Baron wobbles before his belly-slam, so jump his splash waves and stomp him while he is bruised. In Clockwork Fair, conveyor belts carry you left or right, gasket golems need two hits (the first knocks a plate loose), and pistons and lifts are the rides; the Tick-Tock Duchess chimes four times before her pendulum swings, so keep your distance and stomp her after the swing. In Cloudrail Commons, the gaps have no bottom: wind corridors gust a headwind (streaks show when it blows, so jump in the calm), paper kites glide overhead, umbrella mites float down off ledges, and falling cargo crates rest a moment as steps once they land; Admiral Gust's sails fill before his gale pushes you back, so jump the gust wave and stomp him as the wind drops. In Crystal Underworks, crystal bridges are solid only while they glow (they flicker before they fade, so wait for the next glow), puddle mimics rise when you come close, siphon sprites drift after you, and echo gates open when you ring their switch with your wrench; the Prism Mole dives, light points at the spot where it will burst up, so step away and stomp it while it blinks in the open. On the Night Market Rooftops, chimney spirits lob soot in long arcs and tea-kettle knights whistle before they lunge; the Umbrella Dragon snaps its canopy shut and dives low across the arena, so jump over it, and stomp it while it is tangled at the wall. The Stardrain Citadel brings every trick back at once: reservoirs and rafts, belts, valve gates, crystal bridges, wind and falling cargo. In Valveheart, Lady Spigot holds her post; whichever valve hisses is the side her water jet will fire from, so step well clear on that side or stand on a ledge, jump her ring of spray, and stomp her once the jet stops. Free Teo and every river runs home. The toy box on this page previews all fourteen enemy families in their painted animation.

    Eight worlds
    Click an island on the overworld map, or a World chip below it, to scout that world's four stages, its boss and its locals. Arrow keys step between islands. Bluebell Pipeworks, Tideglass Marina, Lantern Orchard, Clockwork Fair, Cloudrail Commons, Crystal Underworks, Night Market Rooftops and Stardrain Citadel culminate in the rescue of the plumber. Painted vignettes replace the drawn scenes as the art pipeline delivers them.

    Casting call
    Meet the eight painted heroines on this page; hover or tap one to preview her. You choose her in the cabinet when you start a new game, and the page then stars your saved heroine on the title screen and the map. Looks are cosmetic: every heroine shares one hitbox and the same abilities.
