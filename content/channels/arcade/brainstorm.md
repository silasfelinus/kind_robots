tutorial:
  title: Show me what you got
  body: |-
    Pick a table or start a round. There are four kinds: Top of the List (terrible ice cream flavors), Who Is This Person (Vanderbuilt Lacrosse owns a time share on Venus), Yes, But (I like Obama, but…) and Crossover (neither Trashcan Jones nor Vanderbuilt Lacrosse has ever paid income tax).

    Write your line and press A. The art modeler paints every line a person writes, and the picture appears on the card when it is done.

    Stuck? Press B. The coach deals a hand of angles to explore, but it never plays for you: you still write the line.

    React with Love it, LOL or Groan. Hot puts the lines the table is enjoying right now first, Top ranks them all time, and New shows the latest. The tables with the best lines rise to the top of the board, and the coach steers toward what your table loves.

    Playing on one device? Tap the people button to seat the table. Each line goes under whoever's turn it is, then the turn passes.
