    Thirty-two stages, one determined heroine.

    Save the Plumber is planned as a full eight-world side-scrolling runner. This Play page is its front door: the title screen, a prologue, the overworld map, the eight heroines, the cast and the Arcade cabinet, where all eight worlds are playable: thirty-two stages, eight bosses and the rescue finale. A new game opens with a painted story of Teo's capture (press A to turn the page, START to skip). Glowing bubbles hold powers: Springstep (higher jumps, and hold A to float down), Bottled Storm (X also throws a gust that strikes foes, turns valves and breaks crates at range) and Pocket Lift (X lifts a crate; X again throws it to knock out a foe, or lands it as a new step). A hit while powered costs the power instead of a heart, and water hearts restore one.

    Movement lab
    Scroll to the cabinet and press Start. Choose New Game, then pick one of eight heroines with the arrows and press A (B picks one at random). Your heroine runs on her own, faster in every world. Press A or Up to jump (hold A to jump higher) and hold B to duck into a slide under beams and gliders; B in the air drops you fast. If a wall pins you for too long, you lose a life. In boss arenas the run stops and Left and Right move you freely. Endless, on the title menu, is a separate run with one life: zone after zone cycles all eight worlds, each one harder and faster than the last, and your distance in metres is the score. Your best Endless run is saved on this device and shown on the title menu and above the cabinet. Jump input is buffered just before landing and remains available for a few frames after leaving a ledge. The cabinet saves your heroine and stage on this device, so Continue picks up where you left off. The Arcade cabinet also manages gamepad, touch, pause and sound.

    Tools and enemies
    Every stage hides one Golden Gear, and each world tucks them away harder: a well-timed jump in World 1, up a step and a ledge in World 2, a two-tier climb by World 3, and narrow single steps over a pit in the last worlds. You grab them on the run, and powers wait on the running line just past the landing. Found gears stay found across runs, and the world map marks them. Every heroine has her own special on Y: Marisol dashes, Pip double-jumps, Tala ground-pounds, Ji-woo shoulder-charges, Odette floats on her parasol, Hodan spins, Priya dodge-rolls and Nova vaults on her running blade. Press X to splash your wrench ahead: it defeats pumpbugs, stuns rivet rollers, breaks crates and turns valve switches, which open blue water gates. Landing on a pumpbug defeats it; landing on a rivet roller stuns it, and touching a stunned roller kicks it into its friends. Steam vents puff on a beat, so time your jump over a hot one, and gliders fly straight at your head in every world: slide under them, stomp them or wrench them. Three hearts protect each life, checkpoint pennants save your place, and turning the goal valve clears a stage. In 1-4, jump Madame Pressure's steam waves and stomp her while she wobbles. In Tideglass Marina, water costs a life, rafts bob on the swell, clockwork minnows leap from the waves, cork crabs scuttle and glass gulls swoop to steal your tokens (stomp one to get them back); Captain Barnaclejack's anchor glows before it sweeps, so jump clear and stomp him while it is stuck. In Lantern Orchard, stomp a moss lantern to flatten it into a step, bounce off spring hares to reach high ledges, and carry your own light through the unlit Rootcellar and Firefly Canopies; the Overripe Baron wobbles before his belly-slam, so jump his splash waves and stomp him while he is bruised. In Clockwork Fair, conveyor belts carry you left or right, gasket golems need two hits (the first knocks a plate loose), and crate stacks block the track until your wrench breaks them; the Tick-Tock Duchess chimes four times before her pendulum swings, so keep your distance and stomp her after the swing. In Cloudrail Commons, the gaps have no bottom: wind corridors gust a headwind (streaks show when it blows, so jump in the calm), paper kites glide in at head height and umbrella mites float down; Admiral Gust's sails fill before his gale pushes you back, so jump the gust wave and stomp him as the wind drops. In Crystal Underworks, crystal bridges are solid only while they glow (they flicker before they fade, so jump the gap when one is dark), puddle mimics rise when you come close, siphon sprites glide in low, and echo gates open when you ring their switch with your wrench; the Prism Mole dives, light points at the spot where it will burst up, so step away and stomp it while it blinks in the open. On the Night Market Rooftops, chimney spirits lob soot in long arcs and tea-kettle knights whistle before they lunge; the Umbrella Dragon snaps its canopy shut and dives low across the arena, so jump over it, and stomp it while it is tangled at the wall. The Stardrain Citadel brings every trick back at once: reservoirs and minnows, belts, valve gates, steam vents, wind and golems. In Valveheart, Lady Spigot holds her post; whichever valve hisses is the side her water jet will fire from, so step well clear on that side or stand on a ledge, jump her ring of spray, and stomp her once the jet stops. Free Teo and every river runs home. The toy box on this page previews all fourteen enemy families in their painted animation.

    Eight worlds
    Click an island on the overworld map, or a World chip below it, to scout that world's four stages, its boss and its locals. Arrow keys step between islands. Bluebell Pipeworks, Tideglass Marina, Lantern Orchard, Clockwork Fair, Cloudrail Commons, Crystal Underworks, Night Market Rooftops and Stardrain Citadel culminate in the rescue of the plumber. Painted vignettes replace the drawn scenes as the art pipeline delivers them.

    Casting call
    Meet the eight painted heroines on this page; hover or tap one to preview her. You choose her in the cabinet when you start a new game, and the page then stars your saved heroine on the title screen and the map. Looks are cosmetic: every heroine shares one hitbox and the same abilities.
