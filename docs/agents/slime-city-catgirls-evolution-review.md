# Gene Riot Catgirls: first full-faction evolution editorial pass

Source: `utils/slimeCity/evolutions/catgirls.ts`, `utils/slimeCity/clashTwists.ts`, and the native Clash card catalog. Implements Conductor slime-city/t-041 as the **first of seven** faction passes.

**Coverage:** 40/40 existing Catgirls cards; 120/120 L1/L2/L3 roles reviewed individually below. Ten original cards use native, hand-built Clash twists; 30 use the evolution DSL. The 40-card inventory is asserted by `verifySlimeCityEvolutions.test.ts`. No new cards, IDs, economies, or art submissions.

**Editorial policy:** Retaining a strong existing behavior after manual review is not the same as inventing a meaningless upgrade to tick a box. The pass includes direct rules-text rewrites for all 30 original-twist descriptions, real ability changes to selected data-driven cards, and a native-logic improvement to Chimera. Level-one roles are **evaluated**, not blindly given extra triggers on top of already-playable base abilities.

## Real-engine acceptance, not just strings

- The evolved **spell** can only fire on casting. In the earlier PR, `Emergency Molt` tried to heal on hit and `Spare Nine Lives` tried to tap, neither possible for a spell card. Both now act on `enter`.
- Evolution tests cover the 40-card, 10-twist, 30-DSL boundary, generated text length and valid triggers by card type.
- Dedicated command-engine fixtures exercise Emergency Molt's cleanse, Secondhand Life's conditional echo, Needle-Thread Ambush's squad keyword, Spare Nine Lives' rescue, and Chimera's neighboring gift.
- The previous 300-card signature diagnostic remains a screening tool, **not** a substitution for the individual review above.
- Meaningful match balance, AI reachability, and multiplayer replay still require completed CI and seeded matchup results. Do not label the entire 900-stage program complete or advance Slime City to playtest solely for this document.
