  title: Slime City · Private Duels
  body: >-
    Two signed-in administrator accounts can battle using private invitations.
    Choose your crew, create a duel and share the invite link. The second player
    chooses a crew and joins. Each fighter sees only their own five-card hand,
    deck counts and armed traps. Your turn: play cards into one of five alleys,
    fire your crew power, or start your attacks; resolve the alleys in order,
    then end your turn. Every card evolves as it is played, up to level three.
    A duel can be reopened using its link after refresh or disconnection.
    These preview battles grant no Meat, cards or other rewards and expire after
    seven days. Do not publish an invite link outside the preview group.
